Before you order

How do you get my size?⌄

After you choose a design, our camera guide helps you take two close-up photos — your fingers, then your thumb — with a coin for scale. It takes about 90 seconds. Mona measures every nail from them.

What if they don't fit?⌄

Your first refit is free. Send us a photo and we'll remake the sizes that don't sit right.

How long do they last?⌄

With good prep, 5–10 days per wear — and each set can be worn 3–5 times. Care instructions come in the box.

How do I pay?⌄

JazzCash, EasyPaisa or bank transfer. Every set is made to measure, so orders are paid in full before we start — you upload a screenshot and we confirm by email.
