Before you order

Good questions.

How do you get my size?

After you choose a design, our camera guide helps you take two close-up photos — fingers, then thumb — with a coin for scale. It takes about 90 seconds, and every nail is measured from them.

What if they don't fit?

Your first refit is free. Send us a photo and we'll remake the sizes that don't sit right.

How long do they last?

With good prep, 5–10 days per wear — and each set can be worn three to five times. Care instructions come in the box.

How do I pay?

JazzCash, EasyPaisa or bank transfer. Every set is made to measure, so orders are paid in full before we begin — you upload a screenshot and we confirm by email.
